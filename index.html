ABout

I’m a front-end developer, and what gets me going is movement. A title that slides in, an image that reveals itself on scroll, a transition that makes you want to keep going: that’s where I have the most fun. GSAP and JS libraries are my best allies.

Before code, there was publishing. That’s where I caught the branding and visual identity bug. Today, I mix the two: I create graphic worlds and bring them to life on screen.

And I love what I do. Every day, I try a new tool or learn a new technique, and it never stops.

Next step: an internship from February to April 2027, in Brussels. If you like websites that move, we should get along.
